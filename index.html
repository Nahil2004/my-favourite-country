日本 — The Land of the Rising Sun

📍 About Japan

Japan is a beautiful island country located in East Asia. It is famous for its rich culture, advanced technology, delicious food, and beautiful natural places.

The capital city of Japan is Tokyo. Japan is known for Mount Fuji, cherry blossoms (Sakura), anime, manga, bullet trains, robotics and traditional temples.

📋 Quick Facts

  • Capital: Tokyo
  • Currency: Japanese Yen (¥)
  • Language: Japanese
  • Population: ~125 million
  • Famous for: Sushi, Samurai, Shinkansen
